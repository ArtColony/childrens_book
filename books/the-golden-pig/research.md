## 1. The main telling (*Choe Goun jeon* 최고운전), beat by beat

The sources do not tell the novel identically. KO-WP gives the fullest version, and the beats below follow it. Where another source differs, it says so. A conflict table follows the list.

1. Silla. Every new magistrate (수령) of **Munchang (문창)** loses his wife; she vanishes. [KO-WP, EK, GS]
2. **Choe Chung (최충)** is posted there. Before the wife disappears he ties a **silk thread (실)** to her hand or wrist. [KO-WP, EK, GS]
3. She vanishes. Choe follows the thread to a **crevice in a rock on the hill behind the town (뒷산 바위틈)**. [KO-WP, EK, GS]
4. At night the rock opens. Inside is a **golden pig (금돼지)** with his wife and the other stolen wives. [KO-WP]
5. The wife tricks the pig into telling its weakness: if **deer hide** sticks to the back of its head, it dies. [KO-WP; EN-WP has the same weakness]
6. Her **pouch cord is deer hide**. She uses it, and the pig dies. [KO-WP, EN-WP] In EK and GS it is simply Choe Chung who goes in and kills the pig. [EK, GS]
7. Husband and wife go home. [KO-WP, EN-WP, EK]
8. In KO-WP **she was already pregnant before she was taken**. A son is born **six months** after the return, with odd-looking nails or toes. [KO-WP]
9. Choe Chung suspects the child is the pig's and **abandons him**. EK: "금돼지의 자식이라며 버렸더니" (he abandoned him, saying he was the golden pig's child). [EK, KO-WP, GS]
10. **Where:** EN-WP says "at the seaside" (uncited). GIMPO says the baby was in the end "thrown into the river (강에다 던져 버렸습니다)". EK, GS and CAFE name no place. A search snippet says "an isolated island (외딴 섬)" (study.zum.com, page would not load; **unconfirmed**).
11. **Protectors:**
    - KO-WP and GIMPO agree that animals (짐승, 가축) keep away from him by day and **celestial maidens (선녀) come at night to nurse him**. GIMPO: "낮에는 가축들이 아기를 피해다녔으며 밤에는 선녀들이 와서 젖을 먹이니". [KO-WP, GIMPO]
    - CAFE: "선녀와 연꽃 및 백조들이 아기를 돌보는 기적" (celestial maidens, **lotus flowers and swans/white birds** care for the baby).
    - EK: a 선녀 descends to protect him, and a **heavenly scholar, 천유 (天儒)**, comes down to teach him to read. GS names only 천유. [EK, GS]
    - SCHO: celestial scholars teach him the classics and composition at about age three.
12. He **teaches himself to read and write** (EN-WP), or is taught by heavenly scholars (EK, SCHO).
13. His mother wants him back. Choe Chung, ashamed, refuses. She spreads word that Choe will only recover from an illness if his son comes home. [KO-WP]
14. Officials are sent, but the boy, now about **three**, refuses to come: his parents threw him away. [KO-WP]
15. Choe gathers the villagers and **builds a pavilion by the sea**. The boy comes, names it **Woryeongdae (월영대, "Moon-Shadow Terrace")**, and studies there with immortals. [KO-WP]
16. One night his recitation carries all the way to China. The Emperor (Tang) sends **two scholars** to test Silla. They meet the boy, lose to him in verse, and go home. [KO-WP, EN-WP, EK] SCHO has him six years old here.
17. Alarmed, the Emperor sends a **sealed box (EN-WP and CAFE: a stone box)** holding **an egg wrapped in cotton**. Silla must write a poem naming what is inside, or be invaded. [EN-WP, KO-WP, CAFE, SCHO]
18. The King offers a reward. The boy, now a youth, goes to the capital as a **mirror-mender**, breaks a mirror at the house of **Minister Na (나업 / 나승상)**, and becomes a servant to pay for it. He tends horses and gardens well and wins the household over. [EN-WP, KO-WP; EK has only the servant in Na Eop's house, with no mirror]
19. The King orders Minister Na to answer the box. The youth will answer **if he may marry Na's daughter (운영)**. Na agrees. [EN-WP, EK, GS] He takes the name **Chiwon**. [KO-WP]
20. His poem says the box holds a **chick**. The egg has hatched inside the box, so the answer is right. [KO-WP, EK, GS, CAFE]
21. The Emperor demands the person who answered. At **twelve** he sails for China after trading farewell poems with his wife. [KO-WP, EN-WP]
22. At sea the ship stalls: the **Dragon King** wants him to teach his son **Yi Mok (이목)**, who then travels with him. Yi Mok makes rain for a drought-struck island, a heavenly monk punishes him for it, and Choe saves him, then sends him home. [KO-WP; EN-WP and EK mention Yi Mok]
23. On the road an **old woman** gives him **cotton soaked in soy sauce**, and a **beautiful woman by a river** gives him **talismans (부적)** (three in EK and GS; "four magic pouches" in KO-WP). [EN-WP, KO-WP, EK, GS]
24. At the Tang court (Luoyang) the Emperor sets traps, including a poisoned meal. The talismans see him through, and the Emperor relents. [KO-WP, EK]
25. He comes **first in the civil-service exam**. [KO-WP, EK, GS]
26. The **Huang Chao rebellion**: he writes one proclamation (토황소격문) and the rebels give up. [KO-WP, EK, GS, CAFE] EK says he is made Marquis of Munsin.
27. Jealous ministers slander him and he is **exiled to a barren southern island**. He lives by sucking the **soy-soaked cotton**. [EN-WP, KO-WP, EK]
28. The Emperor recalls him. He crosses back by a **bridge of dragons** (GS, CAFE) or rides a **blue dragon** (KO-WP). He writes a character on the ground to show that the land is not the Emperor's, and the Emperor gives way or apologises. [KO-WP, EN-WP]
29. He goes home to Silla on a **blue lion (청사자)**. EK: "청사자를 타고 신라로 돌아오나". [EK, GS, CAFE; EN-WP lists it as a variant]
30. In Silla he passes the King's procession without bowing, or is accused of some fault, and is barred from the court. In GS he simply turns down office. [EN-WP, EK, GS] KO-WP: the King admits Choe deserves death, then pardons him for his services. Minister Na has died.
31. **ENDING OF THE MAIN TELLING:** he takes his wife and family to **Mount Gaya (가야산)**, withdraws from the world and **becomes an immortal (신선)**, and is never seen again. EK: "가족들을 데리고 가야산에 들어가 신선이 되었다고 한다." In CAFE he first restores his wife's youth. [EK, GS, KO-WP, EN-WP, CAFE]

**Where the main telling ends:** beat 31. Every source agrees: **he goes into Mount Gaya with his wife and becomes an immortal.** The golden pig appears only in beats 1–9 and is gone after the birth. A picture book that stops at the rescue, or at the boy's homecoming at Woryeongdae (beat 15), is a natural shortening, but it is **not** where the source ends.

**Local versions, plainly.** I found **no golden-pig tale attached to Seonyudo (선유도) itself**. Seonyudo's Choe link is only that he is said to have lived there for a while, drawn by the view of 월영봉 (search snippet, jjan.kr, **unconfirmed**). The Gogunsan pig tale is set on **Naechodo**. A **Woryeongdae on Sinsido (신시도)** is where his flute and recitation reached the Chinese Emperor's ears (jjan.kr 2017, above). For **Munchang**, the novel's setting, the closest local telling I found is the Changwon/Dotseom one, and its only source is a blog. Whether the novel's 문창 is meant as a real place (Changwon/Masan?) is **unconfirmed**. The real Woryeongdae, in 해운동, 마산합포구, Changwon, is where the historical Choe is said to have lived and taught late in life. 경남신문 2016: https://knnews.co.kr/news/articleView.php?idxno=1173430

### The 신라수이전 question

- **Answered.** The Choe Chiwon story credited to 『수이전』 is 쌍녀분 (the two-maidens tomb), not the golden pig (EK E0057712, above). I found **no source** putting the pig birth in 수이전. The 화동인물총기 argument for a pre-1389 date concerns the novel, and whether it covers the pig episode is unconfirmed.

## 4. The historical Choe Chiwon (facts vs legend)

- **Facts:** born 857 (King Heonan 1). Went to Tang at **12 (868)** and passed the 빈공과 exam for foreigners in **874**. Wrote the **Proclamation against Huang Chao (토황소격문, 879)** while on Gao Pian's staff. Returned to Silla in **885**. Served as a provincial governor (Taesan, Cheonryeong, Buseong). Gave Queen Jinseong about ten reform proposals (894), which were blocked by the aristocracy. Withdrew and wandered, living at **Haeinsa on Mount Gaya**. Last attested alive 908; death date unknown. 디지털군산문화대전 「최치원」 (URL above); 경남신문 2016 (URL above).
- **Legend:** the golden pig, the abandonment, the egg in the box, the dragon's son, the magic in China, and the vanishing into Gayasan as an immortal. Hongryu valley lore has him leaving only his hat and shoes. 경남신문 2016.
