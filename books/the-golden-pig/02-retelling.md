# Stage 2: The retelling (draft 1)

*The Legend of the Golden Pig* / *금돼지 전설*. A bilingual retelling of
*Choe Goun jeon* (최고운전) for ages 6–9. The plan is in
[01-interpretation.md](01-interpretation.md).

**Budget:** about 30 English words a page, 38 at most. The Korean has 3
lines in the top band, and the English 4 lines in the bottom band.

**Korean register:** storytelling 해요체 (-었어요, -지요, -답니다), as in
modern Korean picture books. Dialogue is in the speaker's own register.

**Refrain:** *"The golden pig's child, they say."* / *"금돼지의 자식이래."*
It comes on pages 7, 11, 16 and 23b, and is turned on page 28.

## Page turns

- **4 → 5:** what is inside the rock? (the gold cave).
- **7 → 8:** what will the father do?
- **14 → 15 → 18:** what is in the box? It stays unanswered for four pages.
- **19 → 20:** the answer, on the widest page.
- **27 → 28:** the father's line, then the ending.
