# Retelling draft 1: The Golden Pig (Korean reader)

*reviewer-korean persona (run as a general-purpose agent with `.claude/agents/reviewer-korean.md`, since the new agent type loads only in a new session), 2026-11-14. It read the Korean text alone first, for the lesson gate. Saved unchanged.*

**Step 1, the lesson (read from the Korean read-aloud text only)**
- 한국어: "사람들이 수군거리는 말만 듣지 말고, 그 사람이 하는 일을 보고 알아봐야 해."
- English: "Don't judge someone by what people whisper about them. Look at what they actually do."

(The lesson comes through clearly in Korean. Page 27 states it, and the refrain's turn on page 28 makes it land.)

---

**1. Verdict:** Yes, after one pass. The 해요체 is consistent and the 띄어쓰기 is clean: I found no errors. Four spots read like a translation, and one cultural detail (the hat) would sound wrong to a Korean reader.

**2. Line edits**

- **Refrain:** The `-이래` (hearsay) ending is right and matches "they say." Drop the 의 so it reads **"금돼지 자식이래."** People drop it in spoken gossip, and the seven-syllable whisper is punchier. Keep 자식; 새끼 would be too crude. Use the same form all five times.
- **p.1:** The comma structure is stiff. → "옛날 옛적, 신라 문창 고을의 관아 뒤에는 높은 산이 있었어요."
- **p.2:** 최충 is named three times, which is a pronoun-style 번역투. → "…조심성이 많았어요. 잠들기 전, 아내의 손목에 붉은 명주실을 꼭 묶어 두었어요."
- **p.3/4:** Add mimetic words and drop the repeated 바위. → "등불이 훅 꺼졌어요." / "바람이 쌩쌩 불고, 부엉이가 부엉부엉 울었어요. 커다란 바위에 이르자, 실이 바위틈으로 쏙 사라졌어요."
- **p.5:** There are many wives in the cave, so say which one is speaking. → "최충의 아내가 상냥하게 물었어요."
- **p.6:** → "주머니에 달린 사슴 가죽 끈을…꾹 갖다 댔어요." / "새벽에 바위 문이 열리자, 밖에서 최충이 기다리고 있었어요."
- **p.9:** "짐승들도 아기 곁에 오지 않았어요" sounds like the baby was neglected (even the animals stayed away). It should sound like protection. → "바닷가 짐승들도 아기를 해치지 않았어요." The plan shows cranes, so use "하얀 학들이" here and "white cranes" in the English.
- **p.10:** "글을 깨쳤어요" is the idiom; "글자를 깨쳤어요" is not.
- **p.11:** "어머니의 간청으로" and "그들은" are 번역투. → "어머니가 애원하자 사람들이 아이를 데리러 왔어요. 사람들은 "금돼지 자식이래." 하고 수군거렸어요."
- **p.13:** → "목소리가 어찌나 맑은지 바다 건너 당나라까지 들렸어요." This restores "so clear," and 어찌나…지 is classic storytelling Korean.
- **p.17:** The subject of the last sentence is unclear. → "운영은 남들이 뭐라 하든 아랑곳하지 않았지요." Also, **운영** will make Korean adults think of the heroine of *운영전*, a different, tragic classic. Check whether the source names her; if not, consider another name.
- **p.18:** "-으리라 했어요" is archaic. → "답을 내지 못하면 목숨을 내놓으라고 했어요." Add "승상이 물었어요" after "너는 누구냐?"
- **p.19:** "어둠 속에서도 무언가는 변하는 법이에요" is the clumsiest line in the book. → "'황제는 무언가를 넣었을 거야. 하지만 깜깜한 곳에서도 모든 것은 변하는 법이지.'"
- **p.20:** "그 안의 것은 소리를 냅니다" is 번역투. → "처음엔 달걀이었으나, 지금은 소리 내어 우는 병아리입니다." Use "종종걸음으로" instead of 아장아장, which is for toddlers. Use "삐악삐악!" (삐악 is the correct spelling).
- **p.21:** → "열두 살이 된 치원은…" Mark who says each line: "기다릴게요." 운영이 말하자, 치원이 대답했어요. "꼭 돌아올게요."
- **p.22:** "한 할머니" and "한 여인" calque "a." → "길에서 만난 할머니가…", "강가에서는 웬 여인이…", "누가 너를 해치려 하거든 쓰렴."
- **p.23b/25:** 마침내 is used twice. Use 결국 on 23b.
- **p.27:** → "남의 말만 듣고, 너를 보지 않았구나." This is the father's line. An optional alternative, also shorter.
- **p.28:** "모자" sounds wrong. In the 가야산 legend he leaves his **갓** (and shoes). → "소나무 가지에 갓 하나만 걸려 있었지요." "금빛 저녁" is a calque → "노을이 금빛으로 물든 어느 저녁." The turn has a doubled hearsay ("-래" 했지만). → "예전엔 "금돼지 자식이래." 하고 수군거렸지만, 이제는 "신선이 되었대." 하고 이야기한답니다."
- **Names and titles:** 원님, 서라벌, 신라 임금, 당나라 황제, 선녀 and 신선 are all right. 승상 is a Chinese title, but the tale itself uses 나 승상, so keep it. 월영대 is a real 최치원 site in Masan; strictly it is a 대, not a 정자, but that's fine for children.
- **Gloss:** "Chiwon (致遠), reaching far" is correct (致 = bring to, reach; 遠 = far). The back matter should also give his pen name **고운 (孤雲), "lone cloud,"** because it is the 고운 in the title 최고운전 and it echoes the ending in the clouds. Two more back-matter notes: he really was 문창후 (文昌侯), and in the tale he serves as 파경노 ("the mirror-breaking servant"). Sunflowers came from the Americas, so the back matter should explain why they appear in Silla.

**3. Mismatches**
- **p.23b:** The English says the ministers "told the Emperor," but the Korean never says so, which breaks the cause of the exile. Speaking to an emperor in 반말 would also be the wrong register. → "금돼지 자식이래." 수군거림은 결국 황제의 귀에까지 들어갔어요.
- **p.13:** "So clear" is missing in the Korean (fixed above).
- **p.25:** "Home" is missing. → "고향으로 바다를 건넜어요."
- **p.5, 18, 21:** The English says who is speaking; the Korean doesn't.
- **p.12:** Only the English glosses 월영대 (Moon-Shadow Terrace). That difference is fine.

**4. What works**
- 해요체 throughout, with dialogue in each speaker's own register: the pig's "사슴 가죽뿐이지," the boy's formal "제가 답하겠습니다."
- The refrain uses hearsay -래, which matches "they say," and its turn to "신선이 되었대" is very satisfying to read aloud.
- Lines that are good to say aloud: "꽁꽁 봉한 돌함," "무럭무럭," and the bird tracks becoming letters.
