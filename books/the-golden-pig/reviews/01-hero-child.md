*reviewer-child (Haiku), briefed as a 7-year-old, on `01-hero/hero_sheet.png` and `pig_sheet.png`, 2026-11-14. Saved unchanged.*

1. They're the same boy, just at different ages. The label says "the boy: 3, 10, 12." The first one is little and crouching, the next is bigger and reaching out, and the last one is the biggest. Nothing bad is happening to them. They're just getting taller.

2. The lady is the mother. The bundle is the baby, wrapped up in stripes. You can see the baby again lying down at the bottom right, labelled "the baby."

3. The man in the white robe with the tall black hat is Choe Chung. The tall hat with the sticks means he's important. He's pointing with his hand and his face looks a bit cross, so I can't tell if he's nice. The blue man at the bottom left is Chiwon, grown up, and he has the same kind of hat.

4. I like the baby wrapped in stripes best, because it looks like a little yellow-and-teal sausage with a head. Nobody is really scary. The mother's sleeves are a bit funny, and the tiny "smallest" kid is so small I almost missed him.

5. The pigs. The big one is labelled "fierce, the cave." It has a red angry eye and big pointy teeth, so it's scary. The smaller one is "sly, talking," with a little pointy face. The tiny one is "smallest." The upside-down one is "dead, the deerskin." Its legs are sticking up in the air and it has an X on it. It's dead, so it's sad and a bit creepy. It looks like a pig rug, not a real pig.
