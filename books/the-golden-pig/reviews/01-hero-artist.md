# Hero-sheet review: The Golden Pig (artist)

*reviewer-artist (Sonnet) on `01-hero/hero_sheet.png`, `pig_sheet.png` and the chosen style page, 2026-11-14. Saved unchanged.*

I opened style_c.png, hero_sheet.png and pig_sheet.png at full size.

**1. The people**

They read as one hand: same head circle, same dot eyes, same trapezoid bodies. But that is sameness more than family resemblance, and only the colours say Korean.

- **Hanbok.** Mother and Unyeong wear a long tunic over a trapezoid skirt, so the jeogori/chima join is missing. A women's jeogori is short, with the chima tied high under the chest. Add the goreum ribbons, a curved baerae hem, the white dongjeong collar, and a slight bell to the skirt. Sleeves are straight wedges. They need the curved baerae line and a softer sleeve-end curve.
- **Poses.** Every figure is a stiff profile, with one arm out and a lollipop hand. There are no hands or feet that can hold, write or touch. The mother's baby floats at her waist as a striped pill and isn't held. This is the biggest blocker for intimate scenes.
- **The boy.** The braid, red daenggi ribbon and yellow jeogori are right, and he is recognisable at 3, 10 and 12. But the face is the same at every age, with no baby proportions at 3, and bangs plus braid read as a girl. Add a clearer forehead and a topknot-free crown.
- **The boy's yellow.** It sits close to the pig's gold, which is meant to be the book's only gold. Move his jeogori to obangsaek saek-dong or green.
- **Faces.** All the faces are identical. Differentiate them with eyes (long slits for adults, rounder for children), eyebrows, the magistrate's moustache and beard, and Unyeong's smaller face.
- **Hats.** The putou wings are too long and horizontal. Chung and grown Chiwon wear the same hat, so shorten the wings and make Chiwon's black gat or a different rank hat. Note the putou is Goryeo/Joseon, not strictly Silla. A Silla-era look would need a different hat if accuracy matters.
- **Chiwon grown.** He is a recolour of Chung (blue instead of white). Give him a different build and posture.
- **Unyeong.** The pink skirt and yellow jeogori are good. The low bun should carry a binyeo.
- **Intimate scenes.** Not yet. They need three-quarter and frontal turns, bowed heads, kneeling poses, and hands that are not circles.

**2. The pig**

- **Style.** It is a flat, clean cartoon boar in a flat ochre. It is not menacing, and a villain who steals wives needs more than a red eye and one tusk.
- **Shape.** The legs are rectangles, the body is a bean, and the bristles look like a comb. The hooves are black blocks with a yellow band.
- **Gold.** It reads mustard rather than gold. Add paler ochre ring-highlights, gold-dust speckle or leaf-gold texture.
- **Fierce.** Turn the head three-quarters to the viewer, which is the minhwa tiger approach. Make it bigger and wider-eyed, with a high humped shoulder and a lowered head. Add a brow ridge, a visible mouth line with teeth, and two tusks.
- **Sly.** It is the same drawing shrunk down. Narrow the eye, tilt the head, and add a mouth curve.
- **Dead.** On its back with legs up it looks like a comic dead bug. The X eye is cartoon shorthand. Lay it on its side, legs stiff and slightly bent, head turned, with a deer-hide patch visible at the back of the head. The sheet also shows its label overlapping the hoof.

**3. Top 6 fixes, in priority order**

1. Add expressive poses: kneeling, bowing, reaching. Add turnarounds and hands (a small mitten with a thumb is enough) so a mother can hold and a boy can write.
2. Fix the women's hanbok: short jeogori, high-tied chima, curved sleeves and hem.
3. Redo the pig's fierce pose and head (three-quarter, brow, mouth, two tusks, legs tapering to hooves).
4. Move the boy's yellow off the pig's gold, and give the pig a proper gold treatment.
5. Differentiate faces and ages: baby proportions at 3, the beard and moustache on Chung, and a distinct grown Chiwon and hat.
6. Lay the dead pig on its side with the deerskin shown, and fix the label overlap.

**4. Scores**

- Figures: **5/10**. They are consistent and in-palette but too stiff to carry intimate scenes.
- Pig: **5.5/10**. It has an engaging shape but is too cute and mustard-flat for the villain role.
