# Style-study review: *The Golden Pig* (artist)

*reviewer-artist (Sonnet) on the three studies in `01-style/`, 2026-11-14.
The reviewer has no file-writing tool, so its reply is saved here unchanged.*

I opened all three studies and the side-by-side sheet at full size.

## 1. Pick

**Hybrid, built on C.** Paint people as flat minhwa figures (colour inside an ink line) at about 1/3 panel height. Keep A's small-in-landscape scale for the wide, lonely beats. Use B's silhouette only as an occasional device for the moon and climax pages.

- **A:** The figure is about 25 px tall and reads as a speck. It gives a lovely sense of scale, but it can't carry a mother with a baby, a boy writing, or a wedding. It would need a face and a gesture, and it has neither.
- **B:** The silhouette is the strongest single image and the clearest at a glance. It hides faces, so it can't do intimacy. It also only works when a bright moon sits behind the figure.
- **C:** It is the only treatment that scales to faces, hands, a baby's wrap and wedding robes. It is also closest to real minhwa figures (flat colour, ink contour). Its current stiffness is a drawing problem, not a treatment problem.

## 2. Reads as Korean?

Partly. The red thread, gold crack and sunflowers carry the story. The landscape reads as generic "night fantasy", not minhwa.

- **Mountains:** The rounded gumdrop peaks with teal caps are the biggest tell. Minhwa and jingyeong rock is angular and craggy, with stacked, faceted crags and ink texture strokes. Use ridged, serrated peaks in the Geumgangsan or *Ilwol obongdo* manner.
- **Clouds:** The white cartoon puff is Western. Use *sangseo-un* swirls: flat, curled, auspicious cloud bands in white, blue and red.
- **Moon:** Drop the soft glow halo. A minhwa moon is a flat disc, white or gold, with a crisp ring.
- **Pines:** Use layered flat tufts on twisted trunks, not three twigs.
- **Colour:** The deep navy field is the least minhwa choice. Minhwa sits on warm paper or silk ground with bright mineral pigment. Try a lighter indigo wash. Let the obangsaek red, yellow, white, blue and black show in blocks.
- **Patterning:** Add wave or scale pattern in the mid-ground, rock *chon* strokes, and a border of small motifs.

## 3. The figure (C)

- **Hat:** The putou is the main failure. It is a tiny dome with one long black bar stuck out to the left like a pole. A putou has a rounded two-tier crown and two wings (*nalgae*) at the back. With a frontal face, show both wings, or turn the head to profile and show one correctly.
- **Face:** It is too small and blank. The head is about 1/9 of the body, with dot features and no gaze. Enlarge the head, add brow and eye direction, and aim it at the crack.
- **Pose:** He stands upright and frontal with one arm straight out, like a signpost. Nothing says "walking uphill at night". Give him a forward lean, lifted hem, visible feet and the thread wound round his hand.
- **Thread:** It runs behind and then across his robe at hip height, so it looks as if it passes through him. Tie it clearly to his hand or finger.
- **Colour:** The teal robe merges with the teal hills behind him. Move him to a lighter or darker value, or in front of the moon or a pale cloud.
- **Ground:** He stands on a flat, squared dark slab that reads like a stage. Replace it with a path or hillside.

## 4. Layout

It works as a clean bilingual page, and the hierarchy is clear.

- **Spacing:** The English band sits tight under the panel (about 15 px), while the top has more air. Balance the gaps.
- **Korean type:** It looks lighter and smaller than the English. Match optical size and weight, and consider a serif Korean face to match.
- **Frame:** Add a paper mat with a thin double-rule or minhwa pattern border. Let pine branches, cloud tails or the thread break the frame into the margin. Vary panel height by scene: tall for the mountain, short and wide for the town, full-bleed for the climax.

## 5. Top 5 fixes

1. Redraw the mountains as angular minhwa crags with ink texture, and replace the gumdrops.
2. Redraw the figure: correct putou, larger face, a walking pose, a clear thread attachment and a value that separates him from the hills.
3. Swap in minhwa moon and *sangseo-un* clouds, and lift the night from navy to an indigo wash on warm ground.
4. Replace the stage slab with a hillside, and add pattern in the mid-ground.
5. Refine the frame (mat, border, controlled breaks) and the text spacing and type matching.

**Score:** C is 6/10 as drawn. B is 6.5/10 for impact but a dead end for intimate pages. With fixes 1 to 3, the hybrid could reach 8/10.
