# Puff: checkpoint log

*Each gate's questions are yes or no. Every review point is accepted or
declined here, with the reason.*

## Checkpoint 1: ideation

- [x] Three concepts scored on criteria fixed before scoring. **A, Puff:
  24/25** (author's score; the editor called "earned" inflated).
- [x] The message is one sentence a child can say: *No one is too small to
  help.*
- [x] Hero sheet: Puff shows seven distinct moods with no limbs (calm,
  hope, sad, wow, push, thin, joy) at 90 pt and at 44 pt. Also on the
  sheet: the big clouds, the seedling at three stages, and the sunflower.
  Sent to the reader.
- Editor review (6/10), all accepted:
  1. Name *Cloudette* and claim the cost and the return.
  2. The moral goes to the big clouds, the chant to the sunflower.
  3. "Too small" is said three times before it turns.
  4. Soften the cost: rain until the seedling stands, smiling at her
     thinnest.
