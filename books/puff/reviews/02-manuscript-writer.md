- **Spine sound.** The 6→7 turn ("*Plip. Plip. Plip.*" / "And one little leaf lifted up.") is the best in the book. p10 sags: it only captions the picture.

- **Cost and return:** safe and clear; don't touch them.
- **Cut about 60 words the pictures carry:** "High above the hills", "the field grew dry and yellow", "Three little drops", "all over the field", p10's caption, p12's sunflower reveal, and "for a long time".
- **Three changes:**

  2. p13: the clouds rumble "Puff? Too small. Too small." and the sunflower replies "Not too small. Just right."
