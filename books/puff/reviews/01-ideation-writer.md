# Stage 1 ideation: editor (reviewer-writer, Opus)

*Briefed for an explicit-message book, so a stated moral is not a fault in
itself. Summarised from the review. Every point was accepted (see
checkpoints.md).*

**Concept: A is the right pick, but it is not fresh.** The closest
comparable is Tom Lichtenheld's *Cloudette* (2011). In it a small cloud,
left out by the big clouds, rains on a dried-up pond and brings it back to
life. Eric Carle's *Little Cloud*, *Swimmy* and *The Little Engine That
Could* are on the same shelf. A beats B, which is too close to *The Mitten*
and hard to draw, and C, because waiting is not a plot. **Claim the
difference:** Cloudette is never used up, and Puff is used up and refilled.

**The message line.** "No one is too small to help" is clear, and a child
can say it back, but it is a slogan. The sunflower's "Not too small. Just
right." has a beat and answers the refrain, so make it the chant. Put the
plain moral in the **big clouds'** mouths: when the ones who said "too
small" take it back, the line is earned. From the sunflower, it is a
thank-you card.

**Earned?** Mostly. "Three drops lift one leaf" is the best idea in the
document. But the ideation's own 5/5 score is inflated. "Too small" must be
heard **three times** before it turns: from the big clouds, in Puff's head,
and as she starts.

**The water-cycle return** works if it is shown, not taught. One line at
most, and no science words. Otherwise the child's summary becomes "water
goes up and down".

**The wisp is a real risk.** It can read as dying for someone, or as small
girls giving until they are empty. She should rain *until the seedling
stands*, not until she is empty. At her thinnest, her eyes should be open
and she should be smiling, so she reads as tired. Keep the wisp to one
spread, and make the night a warm dusk.

**Suggested 13-page shape:**
1. The sky and Puff.
2. "Too small."
3. The big clouds leave; heat, and a straw-coloured field.
4. The seedling droops.
5. *Too small*, in Puff's head.
6. Three drops, and one leaf lifts.
7. More rain.
8. The seedling stands, and Puff is thin and smiling.
9. Dusk.
10. Mist, and Puff is round again.
11. Weeks later, the big clouds stare.
12. "Not too small. Just right."
13. The big clouds: "No one is too small to help."

**Score: 6/10 as a concept.** It is a well-made, drawable, clearly worded
version of a premise that has been published before. Treating the return
and the reversal as above could take it to 7–8.
