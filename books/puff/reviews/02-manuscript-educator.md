# Stage 2 manuscript, draft 1: educator (reviewer-educator, Sonnet)

- **Vocabulary fits ages 3–6.** *Seedling* and *mist* need the pictures to show them. pp. 11, 12 and 14 are wordy for a 3-year-old.
- **Science is fine as a picture-book truth.** Rain does thin a cloud, and evaporation from wet ground feeds clouds. "The water she gave had come back to her" is a little literal; suggests "...and Puff drank it in". **Draw the mist as soft pale columns, not smoke.**
- **The message is shown before it is said** (pp. 6–9). p14's stated moral repeats p13 in an adult register: consider cutting it. The manuscript's note wrongly says the big clouds say "too small" at pp. 2, 5 and 6; only p2 is theirs.
- **Counting:** three plips equals three drops. **Keep exactly three drops in the art** on pp. 6–7 and the cover.
- **Group read-aloud:** join in on "Too small", "Plip, plip, plip" and "Not too small. Just right." Ask "What could Puff do?" at p5.
- **Recommend:** the class library, ages 4–6.
