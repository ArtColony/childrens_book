# Stage 4 book, first painted pass: artist (reviewer-artist, Sonnet, images)

**Art: 6.5/10.** It is cute, clean and readable, with a sound colour arc.
It is flatter and emptier than *Moss*, and Puff is often too small to carry
the emotion.

**Per-page problems:**

- **Cover:** cross brows; Puff too small; the sun competes with her; the
  subtitle is meta.
- **p1:** the farm is a speck.
- **p2:** rain runs into the mountains; the clouds crowd the text; Puff is
  too small to read.
- **pp. 2→3:** green to desert with no transition.
- **p4:** angry brows; Puff is disconnected from the seedling; heavy leaf
  outline.
- **p5:** the seedling is a 12 px speck.
- **p6:** the seedling is cropped, and the drops miss the leaf.
- **p7:** uneven leaf outlines, random clods, no water on the ground.
- **p8:** the seedling shrinks from p7; the wet patch reads as a pond; the
  rain doesn't reach the ground.
- **p9:** the stem floats with a blunt base, and "stood up tall" is only
  120 px.
- **p10:** "night" with no stars or moon; flat ground.
- **p11:** the mist is five identical columns that read as chimney smoke.
- **p12:** the text is off-centre; only one big cloud; Puff is cramped.
- **p13:** Puff is small and worried at the payoff.
- **p14:** the sunflower collapses from 400 px to 70 px; a dead band;
  "middle." is an orphan.
- **p15:** the rain starts at the rainbow; the rainbow is garish; the
  credit has low contrast.

**Sequence:**

- Almost every page is the same frontal horizon with a small floating
  cloud: no close-up, no top-down view, no spread.
- The hero's scale doesn't follow the feeling.
- The colour arc is the strongest element, and pp. 9–11 are the best
  pages.
- The brow language is inconsistent.

**Versus *Moss*:** it beats Moss on character appeal and expressions, and
on the dusk and mist palette. It loses on foreground layers and texture.

**Top fixes:**

1. Scale: Puff large on the key pages, and a consistent sunflower.
2. Brows: never angled down for Puff.
3. Foreground layers and texture.
4. The mist, the rainbow and the rain; plant the seedling properly.
5. Shot variety; a real night on p10.
