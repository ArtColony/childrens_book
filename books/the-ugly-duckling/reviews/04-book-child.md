# Stage 4 book: cold child reader (Haiku, page images only)

**LESSON:** "Even when you are different and other people are mean, you can keep going and grow up to be wonderful."

- **Story, retold:** the mother duck's eggs crack; one is big and grey; the ducks, the hen and the cat are mean and call him ugly; he runs to the marsh; he swims in a little circle all winter; in spring he flies, three white swans say "Welcome!", and he sees himself in the water: a swan.
- **Favourite:** p16, "a swan on top and its shadow under it, like a mirror. It looks like magic."
- **Confusing:** p13: "dark blue with white dots… I could not tell if it was night or snow or where he was." p5: "tiny italic words I could not read well."
- **Scary or sad:** p7 (dark, rainy, a sad duck by a house with one light); p4 ("How big and ugly he is!"); p6 "all alone"; p8 "no use at all". "Those made me want to hug him."
