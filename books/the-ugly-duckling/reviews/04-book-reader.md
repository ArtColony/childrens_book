# Stage 4 book: cold adult reader (Sonnet, page images only)

**LESSON:** "Being different isn't the same as being bad, and you'll find where you belong while you're still growing." *Gate passed* (with the child's: "Even when you are different and other people are mean, you can keep going and grow up to be wonderful").

- **Confusing, or the picture fights the words:**
  - p7 and p13 are wordless: on p7 it isn't clear the duckling is lost or cold.
  - p5: it isn't clear he is leaving the farm, and the text floats mid-sky.
  - p9: the brown panel on the left third reads as a wall.
  - p12: the dark circle looks like a hole or a button, not water.
  - p14: the dark silhouette against the sun (intended: the reveal waits for p16).
  - The "ugly" duckling is a plain grey bird that looks fine (intended: the yard is wrong).
  - p11: the swans are tiny.
- **Legibility and reading order:**
  - p4: "they said." sits below the quote.
  - p6: "all alone," is tiny in the reeds and easy to miss.
  - p8: "Then you are no use at all!" is clipped by the cat's ear, and the grey "No." lines are hard to read.
  - p11: the curved line runs over the sky.
  - p12: the sentence is split around the circle.
  - p15: a tree trunk overlaps "they".
  - p16: the mirrored text is distracting.
  - p18: the credit line is very small and overlaps the reeds.
- **Worries:**
  - "Ugly", "no use at all", the storm and the winter alone. It is unclear that he survives the winter.
  - Risk of "ugly until you become pretty", only partly answered by p17.
- **Read again?** "Yes, with caveats. The palette is lovely, and the swans on pages 15 to 17 are a satisfying payoff."
