# Stage 4 book: artist (Sonnet, all 19 pages and the contact sheet)

**Score: 7/10.** "The season-by-season light and colour arc is clear and moving, but the finished book is held back by text colliding with art on pp 8, 11, 15, 16 and 18, by uneven duckling scale, and by a flat, repetitive water composition."

**Strongest:** p13 (navy ice, a few snow dots, one grey figure: a quiet wordless page); p14 (the dark silhouette against the pale sun, the "kept grey" beat); p7 (the storm cottage with one lit window).

| Page | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | The mirrored text cuts through the swan's neck and back. The reflection is a pale ghost, not the biggest image | Move the mirrored line below the swan or fade it, and keep it clear of the figure. Make the reflection large and opaque |
| 11 | The rising line touches the duckling's crown. The duckling is about 200 px, bigger than on p12, so growth reads backwards. The far swans are specks | Start the line higher and to the right. Scale the duckling down and enlarge the far swans |
| 8 | "no use at all!" runs into the cat's ear. The hen's tail is cropped. Her legs are thin sticks | Shift the line. Pull the hen in. Thicken her legs |
| 6 | "all alone," is tiny, grey on blue, behind cattails | Place it in open water or sky, larger and dark |
| 2 | "Crack!" floats on dark leaves with low contrast, and the big egg has no crack | Put "Crack!" by the egg in cream. Note: the big egg is meant to stay whole on p2 ("the biggest egg did not crack") |
| 5 | The bird is a small dot mid-lawn, not running under the hedge. The second sentence touches the hedge top | Move the bird lower left and enlarge it. Lift the text |
| 15 | "Welcome," is cut by a trunk. The swans are small | Move the line left. Enlarge the swans |
| 18 | The credit box sits on the reeds, the type is under 8 pt, near the trim | Move the credits to a plain strip, clear of reeds |
| 0 | The title ascenders are about 5 px from the trim. The banded sky reads as scan-lines | Lower the title. Soften the bands |
| 9 | The brown slab reads as a wall, not a doorway | Add a door shape or light spill |
| 12 | Ice-crack marks cross the text | Keep the marks out of the text zone |
| 17 | A five-line text block. The swans are cropped, and none stands out as the hero | Enlarge the swans and make the young one slightly bigger |

Across the book, the sparkly horizon band repeats on pp 3, 6, 9, 15, 16 and 17. The duckling's ear tufts on pp 4, 5 and 8 read as cat ears.
