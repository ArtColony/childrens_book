# Review round 2: artist (Sonnet, fresh, page images and the page plan only)

**Score: 6.5/10.** "The seasonal colour arc and the text-led sequence are strong. The duckling is an expressionless, same-pose lump, and the emotional beats on pp 5, 7, 9 and 16 are too small or too pale."

**Single biggest lift:** "give the duckling expression and scale on the emotional pages (pp 5, 7, 9, 16) and a swan-white-on-dark silhouette on p16."

**Strongest:** the cover, p11, and p12 with p13.

| Page | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
