# Finished-book review: illustrator and art director

*`reviewer-artist` on Sonnet. Given the 19 rasterized pages and the page plan. File numbers p-NN = printed page NN−1.*

**Overall.** The counting pages read clearly, and the light moves forward through morning, gold, sunset, dusk, fog and night. The weak points:
- the lamp gold doesn't win on the light fog pages;
- **Ana doesn't read as holding the lamp in both hands** (the far arm hides behind her body);
- the reunion (p-16 to p-18) is drawn too small.

**Mama is visible on every fog and night page: pass.**

| Page | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| p-01 | Lamp one-handed; dead space between title and figures | Two hands; bigger Ana |
| p-02 | Ten boats a tiny clump at the far right | A clear staggered line |
| p-04–08 | Near-identical compositions; foreground boats under the page number; dark text on mid-violet | Raise the boats; cream text on p-06 |
| p-08 | Hook, lamp and net gone; gulls read as petals; half the page empty | Restore them; draw gull shapes with wings |
| p-09 | The fog puff is a white ball (cloud or smoke) | Horizontal, low-opacity bands |

| p-11 | Arm over the face; post through the hood | Redraw |

| p-14 | The "biggest light" is muddy mustard-khaki; flat-footed | Brighter rings, a hot core, a darker surround; heels lifted |
| p-15 | The blue boat reads grey; its blink is weaker than Ana's glow | Make the boat's lamp the brighter point |
| p-16 | **A second lamp on the hook while Ana holds one** (draft 1's bug); the blue boat is about 30 px in a corner | Remove it; bring the boat and Grandpa up |
| p-17 | **Wrong time of day:** a lighter lavender sky breaks the descent into night | Deep indigo |
| p-18 | The boats are a sticker strip; no grin; adults cut off | Overlap and scale; show the grin |
| p-19 | Figures tiny, not reading as walking | A stride |

**Three changes:** fix the lamp (two hands, a luminous feathered glow, no second lamp); fix the reunion pages (night tone, scale); fix the quiet pages (hook and net, real gulls, a worried face, banded fog).
