# Finished-book review: child

*`reviewer-child` on Haiku, a fresh reader. Given only the 19 page images rasterized from the print PDF.*

1. **Favourite:** "Out in the fog, something blinks back!" (p14). "I like the little blue boat with its light."
2. **Scary:** "The fog swallows the boats. It swallows the pier" (p9) and "Nothing. Only fog." (p12). "Ana looks very small, and where's Grandpa?" It got better when the blue boat blinked back and Grandpa lifted her up. *[The p9 close-up is intense for bedtime; read it calmly.]*
3. **Words I don't know:** *nets, mends* (p1), *gulls* (p7), *swallows, whispers* (p9). "Ten is fog" makes no sense to me, because fog is not a number.
4. **Too long:** p2 ("She waits for one most of all. It is little, and it is blue…"). "p7 is short but I was waiting for something to happen."
5. **Pictures and words:** p7 says "Ana waits for ten", but I see three gulls and no boats. **p17 says "Eleven", and I counted ten boats in the row.**
6. **Page turns:** "On p3, 'One!' I wanted to see the next boat. On p8, 'Ten is fog!' I wanted to turn quickly to see if the boats came back. On p12 I said 'Where's Grandpa?' I always wanted to turn the page at the lamp pages."
7. **Again tomorrow?** "Yes! I like counting the boats, and I like the little light at the end. But I'd ask, 'Why is ten fog? And why is it eleven?'"
