**Global value problem.** Fog and night pages (8–14) are pale grey (L about 75%), and the gold is a beige disc on grey. *Light only reads against dark.* Step the values down from p8 to a cool blue-grey (L about 40%) by p12–14. Then the lamp is the lightest, warmest shape and the white text is legible.

**Top three changes**
1. **Rebuild p13 as the book's biggest image.** Push in so Ana is about 170 pt, on tiptoe, both arms straight up, lamp well above p12's. The brightest gold in the book on dark fog, with Mama small and lit beside her. The headline in the dark band.
2. **Make "Ten is fog" literal (p8).** A soft bank standing in the tenth slot, carried into p9 as the vanishing boats.
3. **Fix value, text and safety on 9–17.** Darken the fog-night pages, put the text on dark, put Mama in every frame, and give Ana both hands on the lamp.
