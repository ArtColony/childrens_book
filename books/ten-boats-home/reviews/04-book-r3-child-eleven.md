# Final check: child counts the "Eleven" page

*`reviewer-child` on Haiku, a fresh agent, shown only the last three pages after round 2's p17 fix. Asked to count the row.*

"One red! Two green! Three yellow! Four white! Five pink! Six green! Seven purple! Eight brown! Nine orange! Ten blue! Then the glowy light on the pole, that's eleven! So there are eleven things in the row, but only ten are boats. Eleven is one more than ten. Grandpa said 'Ten boats home' on the last page, so why does Ana say eleven? **Did she count the light?** I think the light is not a boat! Can we turn the page now?"

*[The row holds 10 boats plus 1 lantern on a post, 11 things in all. The child works out that Ana counted the light, which is the intended joke. "How many boats? How many lights?" is a good question for the grown-up to ask.]*
