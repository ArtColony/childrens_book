# Manuscript draft 2 review: child (fresh reader)

*`reviewer-child` on Haiku, a new agent that had not read draft 1. Given only the read-aloud text.*

1. **Favourite page?** "The counting! Red sail, ONE! Green sail, TWO! And then the Grandpa-not-Grandpa ones, I like saying them with you." *[A counting refrain gives a child a chant to join in on.]*
2. **Scary?** "Yes. The fog. 'How will he find us in all this grey?' And then 'Nothing. Only grey.' I was worried Grandpa was lost. But then the little light blinked back and it was Grandpa's boat, so it got better." *[A real fear, resolved within one turn.]*
3. **Didn't understand?** "What's a pier? And what are nets? Why does Mama mend them? And why is ten fog? Ten is a number, not fog." *["Ten is fog" is a metaphor a five-year-old takes literally. It needs a visual cue.]*
4. **Too long?** "The first page is too long. It's a long wiggly sentence and I lost my place. The bit with the lamp, 'Mama lights it and shuts its little glass door, Ana holds it in both hands,' is long too. I wriggled."
5. **Pictures:** none shown.
6. **Page turns:** "At 'Ana waits for ten,' I wanted to turn it right away to see if ten comes. At 'Ten is fog' I said, wait, what? At 'So she holds it higher' I turned it fast because I had to see the light. Yes, every page."
7. **Again tomorrow?** "Yes! For the counting and for the blue boat blinking. And for Grandpa saying 'this one is mine.' I want to say 'Ten!' really loud."

*Summary:* strong and re-readable. The issues are the long sentences on p1 and the lamp page, unfamiliar nouns (pier, nets), and the "ten is fog" metaphor for a literal listener.
